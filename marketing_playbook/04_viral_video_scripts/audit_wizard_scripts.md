## Executive Overview: The "Audit Wizard" Hook Strategy

Traditional budgeting apps fail because they cause **decision paralysis**: users open an overwhelming dashboard with 40 line items, feel guilty about their spending, and close the app without taking action. Furthermore, standard budgeting tools contaminate cancellable SaaS with fixed utility bills (electric, rent, car insurance) that cannot be impulsively canceled.

The **SubTracking Audit Wizard** solves this through two powerful product mechanics:
1. **Smart Discretionary Filter**: Automatically isolates discretionary subscriptions from fixed bills (`subscriptions.filter(s => !s.isEssential && s.category !== 'Utility Bills')`).
2. **Binary Tinder-Style Gamification**: Presents subscriptions one card at a time with Framer Motion fluid physics. Users swipe **Left to Toss** (cancel) or **Right to Keep**, culminating in an immediate celebration screen (`PartyPopper`), real-time monthly and annual savings tallies, and an exported cancellation CSV checklist.

These 3 scripts turn subscription decluttering into an addictive, satisfying mobile game that viewers want to play immediately.

## Production Cheat-Sheet for Creators (Audit Wizard Series)

1. **Card Physics are King**: When recording the mobile screen, ensure the card rotation (-20° left for Toss, +20° right for Keep) is clearly visible. The visual physics of the card flying off-screen triggers immediate mirror neurons in the viewer's brain.
2. **Audio Syncing**: Sync the swipe sound effect (`whoosh`) exactly to the frame where the card leaves the center stack. Unsynced audio ruins the tactile illusion.
3. **Show Real Amounts**: Viewers ignore generic "$10" amounts. Use realistic, recognizable pricing: Netflix ($15.49), Spotify ($11.99), Adobe CC ($54.99), Planet Fitness ($24.99), iCloud ($2.99).
4. **Lighting**: If filming face-to-camera, use a 45° softbox key light with a cool blue or purple background rim light to match SubTracking's modern dark-mode aesthetic.
