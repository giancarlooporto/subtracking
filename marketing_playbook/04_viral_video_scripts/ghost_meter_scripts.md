## Executive Overview: The "Ghost Meter" Hook Strategy

The average consumer estimates their monthly subscription commitments at **$86/month**, while actual bank statements average **$219/month** (C+R Research). Even more critically, humans suffer from a severe **time-horizon cognitive bias**: a $15.49/month Netflix subscription is mentally categorized as a trivial impulse purchase ("just two lattes"). 

The **Ghost Meter** breaks this illusion by projecting the cumulative nominal cost over 5 and 10 years (`monthly * 12 * 10`), revealing that ordinary streaming habits cost **$1,800 to $3,500+** in cash—and upwards of **$5,000+** in lost compound investment opportunity.

These 3 scripts are engineered to exploit this dissonance in the first **1.5 to 3 seconds**, driving high retention, repeat loops, emotional comment debates, and immediate app opens.

## Production Cheat-Sheet for Creators (Ghost Meter Series)

1. **The 3-Second Rule**: Never start with "Hey guys, today I want to show you an app." Start directly with a shock number or a visceral conflict ("Netflix isn't $15", "Never give apps your bank password").
2. **Text Placement Safety Zones**:
   - Keep all text overlays between **15% from the top** and **35% from the bottom** of the 9:16 screen.
   - Avoid the right edge where TikTok/Reels heart, comment, and share buttons overlay.
   - Avoid the bottom 20% where captions, audio names, and profile handles sit.
3. **Ghost Meter Color Grading**:
   - Set SubTracking to **Dark Mode** (the default high-contrast theme).
   - The red accent color on the 10-Year Lost Wealth counter contrasts sharply against the dark background, making it pop on mobile displays even under bright sunlight.
