As corporate profit targets escalated, Rocket Money instituted controversial monetization changes that have created massive churn and user resentment:

1. **Aggressive Price Hikes & Slider Traps**: Rocket Money’s "Pay What You Think Is Fair" slider defaults users into recurring fees between **$7 and $14 per month ($84 to $168 per year)**, with Premium+ climbing to **$15/month ($180/year)**. Paying up to $180 annually just to monitor recurring bills directly contradicts the goal of saving money.
2. **Aggressive Bill Negotiation Cuts**: Rocket Money charges between **35% and 60% of the first year’s estimated savings upfront** for automated bill negotiations—often charging users for reductions they could have secured with a 5-minute customer support chat.
3. **Plaid Security & Privacy Concerns**: Rocket Money mandates full read-and-scrape access to user bank accounts via Plaid. Plaid’s $58 million federal class-action settlement confirmed that financial aggregators collect and monetize vast troves of personal transaction history.
4. **Account Cancellation Friction**: Users routinely complain about confusing dark patterns when trying to downgrade from Rocket Money Premium to free.

SubTracking intercepts frustrated Rocket Money users with an unbeatable counter-offer: **stop paying a subscription to track subscriptions**. With SubTracking, you get a **100% Free Local Vault** with **zero bank logins**, complete on-device privacy, and an optional **$8.99/year Pro Pass** for multi-device encrypted cloud sync.
