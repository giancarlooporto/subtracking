## 2. Competitive Context & Psychological Trigger

When Intuit permanently shuttered Mint on March 23, 2024, it forcibly funneled over 3.6 million active users into Credit Karma. Rather than preserving Mint’s core budgeting, net worth tracking, and subscription auditing features, Credit Karma stripped out manual categorization, eliminated granular subscription reminders, and transformed user feeds into aggressive lead-generation funnels for high-APR credit cards, auto refinancing, and personal loans.

Former Mint users are experiencing **"Aggregator Betrayal"**:
1. **Ad Saturation**: Users are treated as advertising inventory rather than customers.
2. **Bank Sync Fragility**: Constant multi-factor authentication (MFA) prompts, broken API tokens, and sync delays create maintenance fatigue.
3. **Data Harvesting Apprehension**: Heightened awareness of financial data brokering following Plaid's $58 million class-action settlement for unauthorized transaction scraping.

SubTracking positions itself as the **antidote to aggregator fatigue**: a streamlined, intentional, privacy-first subscription tracker that requires **zero bank logins**, runs 100% locally on your device for free, and offers optional cross-device encrypted sync for just $8.99/year.
