2. **Educational Mandate**: Ad Grant campaigns must direct traffic exclusively to non-commercial educational content:
   - Consumer education on dark pattern subscription traps (ROSCA violations).
   - Interactive calculators teaching the mathematics of opportunity cost and compound inflation.
   - Open-source cancellation letter templates for gym and telecommunications contracts.
3. **Google Ad Grants Operating Constraints**:
   - **Maximum Bid**: Bids were historically capped at $2.00 Max CPC unless using "Maximize Conversions" automated smart bidding.
   - **Minimum CTR**: Accounts must maintain an overall Click-Through Rate (CTR) of **at least 5.0%** across each month; falling below 5% for two consecutive months triggers account suspension.

   - **No Single-Word Keywords**: All keywords must consist of two or more words (e.g., `subscription tracker`, not just `subscriptions`).
   - **Active Account Governance**: Must complete the annual Google for Nonprofits survey and maintain active conversion tracking via Google Analytics 4.
